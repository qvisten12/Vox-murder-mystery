## Production notes

- **Respect the victims:** skip the dashcam impact clips and the cockpit audio. Text cards and maps carry the story without sensationalizing it.
- **Hedge the cause:** the stall/overspeed graphic explains the physics generally. Don't present it, or the elevator evidence, as the confirmed cause.
- **Vox look:** paper-texture backgrounds, a yellow highlighter on key phrases, annotated maps, slow zooms on stills.
- **Optional final line** if the preliminary report is out before you publish: replace the last VO line with its key findings.

## Key facts checklist

- Date/time: Sunday, Nov 1, 2026, \~12:50 p.m. EST
- Aircraft: Learjet 60, N722BD (2004), operated by Aurora Aviation dba Upper Jets under Part 135
- Route: Austin-Bergstrom (TX) → North Eleuthera (Bahamas)
- On board: 9 (7 passengers, pilot, copilot), no survivors
- Location: Military Trail near 10th Ave N, Greenacres area, Palm Beach County
- Crew calls: ice and turbulence, then a mayday; descent of 10k → 20k (overspeed) → 22k ft/min, 60° bank
- Investigation: CVR recovered; NTSB examining possible separation of the left elevator before impact
