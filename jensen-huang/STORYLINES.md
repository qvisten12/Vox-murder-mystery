# Storylines: The Jensen Huang scandal (Vox-style, ~90 s, 1920x1080, HeyGen voice + catalog music)
One idea: **why one CEO's affair became a US-China national-security crisis, and cost him the company he built.**

## A. The Chip in the Middle (recommended: answers the "why")
- Device / form: the object is the explanation. A flight through one world. One Nvidia GPU (drawn as a chip die) sits at the centre of a map; the camera keeps returning to it.
- What you see: the chip is the most valuable object on Earth. Lines from it run to Washington (export rules), China (H20 → H200), Singapore (the Megaspeed probe) and one person, Alice Huang, marked as "suspected asset". When the affair surfaces, every line pulls tight on the man holding the chip. Then he's gone, and the chip stays.
- First 2 s: a single chip die fills the frame; the type reads "$4.5 TRILLION COMPANY". A red thread snaps taut across it.
- Beats: hook (the most valuable chip, the most powerful CEO) → why chips are geopolitics → the suspected spy and the memo → 8 Nov confession → the cascade (shares, Congress, more reports) → 12 Nov resignation → the open question (spy or not? comeback?).
- Look: Vox map collage. Warm newsprint-grey paper, black ink, Nvidia-green as the single accent on the chip only, red for the threads. Condensed sans with a mono for dates.

## B. Nine Days
- Device / form: steps in order. One continuous dolly along a timeline wall from 29 Oct to 16 Nov. A stock line draws underneath like a seismograph.
- First 2 s: "OCT 29" flips over on a split-flap date board, and the seismograph needle twitches.
- Beats: one stop per date. The needle spikes at 8 Nov and 12 Nov, and the board ends on "?" for the comeback.

## C. The Empty Jacket (the bold one)
- Device / form: one frame that rebuilds. The iconic black leather jacket hangs on a chair at a keynote stage. Headlines, memos and stock tickers pile up around it, and the stage dismantles itself, piece by piece.
- First 2 s: a spotlight on an empty leather jacket on a chair, and a teleprompter reads "I was unfaithful…".
- Beats: keynote glory → the memo → the letter → the stage strips down → an empty chair labelled INTERIM.

All three: HeyGen voice "Jennifer – Smooth & Warm" (alt: "Wade – Resonant & Narrative"), HeyGen catalog music, captions, motion blur on, every move on long ease-in-out curves.
