## Timeline
- Oct 2025: US and Singapore probe Megaspeed over possible export-control evasion. Alice Huang (no relation) is linked to it. [TC][WP]
- Dec 2025: Trump allows H200 sales to China ("roughly six times more powerful" than the H20). [AJ][TC]
- Spring 2026: memo drafted under Rubio identifies Alice Huang as an MSS (China's Ministry of State Security) asset. [WP][TC]
- 29 Oct 2026: NYT reports US officials concluded she was a Chinese intelligence asset, and that Huang got no "defensive briefing". [WP][TC][AJ]
- 5 Nov: House chairmen (Moolenaar, Mast, Crawford) request a closed-door briefing from Huang. [TC]
- 7 Nov: FT reports US officials believe Huang had a romantic relationship with her. [WP]
- 8 Nov: Huang's letter to staff: "I was unfaithful to my wife. My actions hurt her and our children. There is no excuse for what I did. I am deeply sorry." [TC][AJ][WP]
  The board starts an independent investigation and sees "no reason to believe national security or Nvidia secrets had been compromised." [WP][AJ]
- 8 Nov: Trump: "I just know he's a fine person, but you never know what's going on." [TC]
- 9 Nov: shares fall. AJ says "more than 4%" overnight; TC says "over 5%". **Say "about 5%" or avoid the figure.**
  Trump calls Huang an "honourable guy". [AJ]
- 9 Nov: China: "nothing but smears against China." (Guo Jiakun) [AJ]
- 11 Nov: NYT reports a relationship with an Nvidia executive who reported to him (not confirmed). The WSJ says the board is preparing to remove him. [WP][TC]
- 12 Nov: Nvidia announces Huang "resigned as president, chief executive officer and a director, effective immediately". CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO. [WP][TC]
  Reuters reports a relationship with another employee. [WP][TC] TC says shares were down 11% over five days. [TC only]
- 12 Nov: the AG says the DOJ has reached no conclusion on whether Alice Huang was a spy. CNN says the intelligence "did not prove" it. [WP only]
- 14 Nov: an open letter asking the board to reinstate him draws ">178,000 signatures within four days". [WP]
- 15 Nov: FT reports Huang is weighing a comeback. [WP]
- 16 Nov: CNBC, WSJ and BBC report the board is not considering reinstatement. Trump says he doesn't know if she "was actually a spy". [WP]
- Testimony: Huang agreed to a closed-door House interview "before Thanksgiving" (Mast, per TC). Dates conflict across sources, so say "agreed to talk to Congress".
