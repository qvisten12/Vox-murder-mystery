# Storylines — The Incredible Story of the Cartel Olympics
Vox-style explainer · 1920×1080 · ~80–100 s · one idea: **the best cartel story of the year was a cover story for a prison sentence.**

## A. The Evidence Board (recommended: the con *is* the explainer)
- **Device / form:** the object is the explanation. One frame rebuilds: a single collage evidence board the camera never leaves.
- **What you see:** act one pins up the legend piece by piece: van, windowless room, tattoo, scoreboard, gold medal. Act two pulls each pin out, stamps it, and pins the real document in its place, until the board reads as a fraud case.
- **First 2 s:** a torn paper Olympic-rings tattoo slams onto the board, and a typed strip lands under it: "If you win, you live. If you lose, you die."
- **Beats:** hook → the tale (9 Feb 2023, Don Paco, the tournament, overtime gold) → who bought it (talent manager → movie star → magazine, 8 months) → first crack (no Olympic record, photo is someone else) → the real 9 Feb 2023 (fraud arrest, $700k, Beatles/Yoko, 18 months) → reframe: the prison became the "cartel prison" → end line: "The movie is back on."
- **Look:** photocopied halftone cutouts on raw corrugated cardboard, black and kraft brown. Red string and stamps are the only accent. Condensed bold sans.
- **Sound:** a low pizzicato-and-tick underscore that drops out at the reveal, with paper, pin, tape and stamp sounds on every action.

## B. One Date, Two Stories
- **Device / form:** the same date told twice. Split screen that collapses into one.
- **What you see:** a calendar page for 9 February 2023 tears in half. The left half follows Mau's story, the right half the public record. They run in sync until the arrest warrant slides across the seam and the two halves fuse into one.
- **First 2 s:** a calendar page, 9 FEB 2023, splits down the middle under the question "Where was Mau Morales on February 9?"
- **Beats:** the question → left: kidnapping, tournament, medal / right: blank, redacted → the believers → right side fills: warrant, prison, $700k → merge → end.
- **Look:** ledger and calendar paper, a grid, a stamped-date typeface. Gold for the claim, red for the record.
- **Sound:** a clock-tick rhythm that splits into two tempos and rejoins.

## C. The Trailer That Never Was (the bold one)
- **Device / form:** result first. Cut on the beat, then the reel breaks.
- **What you see:** the first 20 s are a punchy original movie-trailer pastiche of Mau's story: no studio logos, no real faces. Then the film strip jams, and a fact-checker's red pen marks up every frame of the trailer we just watched.
- **First 2 s:** a black-and-gold title card, "BASED ON A TRUE STORY", and the words "TRUE STORY" get circled in red.
- **Beats:** trailer (kidnap → the games → overtime → gold) → reel jams → markup, frame by frame → the real story → "Coming soon? It might be."
- **Look:** film-strip frames and trailer type, then red-pen annotation over freeze frames.
- **Sound:** trailer braams and hits that cut out to silence, then a pen-scratch rhythm.
